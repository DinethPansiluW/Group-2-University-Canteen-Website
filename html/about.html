Taste of Ruhuna brings every university canteen together in one place—helping students check live meal availability, compare prices, and pre-order between lectures.

Our Mission

To eliminate long queues during tight lecture breaks by giving University of Ruhuna students a fast, transparent way to browse daily menus and reserve meals ahead of time.

Supporting Campus Canteens

By helping canteen operators track real-time demand and update stock status effortlessly, we reduce daily food waste while ensuring fresh meals are ready when you arrive.
